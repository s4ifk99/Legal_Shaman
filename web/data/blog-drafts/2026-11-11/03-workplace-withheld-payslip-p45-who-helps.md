## Summary

If a **workplace** withheld your final payslip or P45, who helps starts with a written request to payroll and HR, then ACAS early conciliation where pay is disputed, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** force payroll to issue documents as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep leaving date, last payslips, and any email asking for the P45.
- Missing P45 can block a new starter tax code; HMRC can help with emergencies.
- Unpaid final wages are a separate money claim path from document chasing.

## What the law says in England and Wales

This section explains the general law. It is not advice on your situation.

- Employees and workers have a right to an itemised **payslip** under section 8 of the **Employment Rights Act 1996**. It must show gross pay, deductions and net pay.
- If an employer does not provide payslips, an employment tribunal can declare what they should have shown, and may order repayment of unnotified deductions.
- HMRC PAYE rules require an employer to give you a **P45** when you leave. If it does not arrive, HMRC can help with your tax code.
- Before most tribunal claims you must contact ACAS for early conciliation. Time limits are short, usually three months less one day.
