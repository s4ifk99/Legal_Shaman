## Summary

If a **workplace** withheld your final payslip or P45, who helps starts with a written request to payroll and HR, then ACAS early conciliation where pay is disputed, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** force payroll to issue documents as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep leaving date, last payslips, and any email asking for the P45.
- Missing P45 can block a new starter tax code; HMRC can help with emergencies.
- Unpaid final wages are a separate money claim path from document chasing.
