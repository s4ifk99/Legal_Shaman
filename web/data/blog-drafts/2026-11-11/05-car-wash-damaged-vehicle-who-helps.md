## Summary

If a **car wash** damaged your vehicle, who helps starts with photos, a dated complaint to the operator, then your insurer if needed, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** negotiate the repair for you as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Photograph damage before you leave the site if you can do so safely.
- Keep the receipt, lane number, and any CCTV request in writing.
- Insurance excess and no claims bonus may make a small claim path more sensible than a claim.

## What the law says in England and Wales

This section explains the general law. It is not advice on your situation.

- A car wash is a service. Under the **Consumer Rights Act 2015** a business must carry out a service with reasonable care and skill.
- If the **car wash damaged your car** through a lack of care, you can ask the business to put it right or pay the cost of repair.
- A sign saying the business is not responsible for damage cannot remove that duty. The Act stops traders excluding liability for failing to use reasonable care.
- Photos taken on site, the receipt and a repair quote are the key evidence. If the business refuses, the small claims track of the county court is the usual next step.
