## Summary

If a **mobile** network quotes an early exit fee you think is wrong, who helps starts with the network complaint, then Alternative Dispute Resolution where the network is signed up, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** cancel the contract for you or sue the network as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep the contract start date, notice period, and the written exit fee quote.
- Ask for a full breakdown of handset subsidy versus airtime.
- Ofcom rules and ADR schemes cover many UK mobile providers.

## FAQ

**They say the fee is non negotiable.**

Ask for the clause and the calculation in writing. Compare it with your remaining airtime and handset balance before you escalate.
