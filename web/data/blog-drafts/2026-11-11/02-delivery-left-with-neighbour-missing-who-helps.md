## Summary

If a **delivery** was left with a neighbour and then went missing, who helps starts with the seller and courier complaint trails, then your card issuer where you paid by card, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** retrieve the parcel for you or sue the courier as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep tracking screenshots, delivery photo, and neighbour name if known.
- Contract is usually with the seller, not only the courier.
- Safe place instructions you never agreed can matter for liability arguments.
