## What the law says in England and Wales

This section explains the general law. It is not advice on your situation.

- If you bought from a business, the **Consumer Rights Act 2015** says goods must be delivered within the agreed time, or within 30 days if no time was agreed.
- If the seller misses that deadline you can usually set a further reasonable deadline and then cancel for a refund.
- Buying from a private individual gives fewer rights. The claim is mainly in contract, and most help comes from the platform's own buyer protection.
- Paying by card may let you ask for a **chargeback**, or claim under **Section 75** for credit card purchases over £100 and up to £30,000.
