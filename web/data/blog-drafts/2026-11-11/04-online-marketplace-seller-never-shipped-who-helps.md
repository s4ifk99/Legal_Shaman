## Summary

If an **online marketplace** seller never shipped, who helps starts with the marketplace buyer protection clock, then your card or PayPal dispute tools, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** chase the seller for you as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Screenshot the listing, chat, payment receipt, and protection deadline.
- Marketplace resolution and card chargeback can run on different clocks.
- Private sellers and business sellers can have different Consumer Rights Act coverage.
