## Summary

If a **boiler engineer botched the repair** and will not fix it, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 says a service must be done with reasonable care and skill. You can ask the trader to put it right, and if they will not, ask for some money back. If you think the work is unsafe, contact Gas Safe Register straight away. The free doors below come from Legal Shaman Matching Help. They will **not** chase the trader or send someone to fix it.

## Key Information

- If you smell gas or think the boiler is unsafe, turn it off and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999 first.
- Under the **Consumer Rights Act 2015**, you can ask the trader to redo the work at no extra cost. If that is impossible or not done in reasonable time, you can claim a price reduction.
- Gas engineers must be on the Gas Safe Register. Check the engineer's ID number and report unsafe or unregistered work.
- Get a second engineer's written report on what went wrong. It is key evidence.
- If you paid by card, ask your bank about a chargeback, or Section 75 if you paid between £100 and £30,000 on a credit card.

## Practical Guidance

- Write to the **trader** describing the fault and asking them to put the repair right within a set time.
- Check the engineer on the **Gas Safe Register** and report unsafe work.
- If they refuse, ask your card issuer about **chargeback** or **Section 75**, and keep the second engineer's report.

## FAQ

**The engineer says the new fault is unrelated. What now?**

Ask for that in writing and get an independent report. If the report links the fault to the repair, send it to the trader.

**Can I get another engineer and charge the first one?**

Usually you should give the trader a chance to fix it first. If they refuse or fail, you may be able to claim the reasonable cost of putting it right.

**Is a botched boiler repair a safety issue?**

It can be. If you are worried, switch off and call the gas emergency line, then report the engineer to Gas Safe Register.
