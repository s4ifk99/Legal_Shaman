## Summary

A **letter before action** is the last formal letter you send before starting a **small claim**. Courts expect it, and skipping it can cost you even if you win. Who helps: free templates and advice, then the online claim service if they still do not pay. The free doors below come from Legal Shaman Matching Help. They will **not** send the letter or issue the claim for you.

## Key Information

- The Practice Direction on Pre Action Conduct expects you to set out the claim, the amount, and what you want, and to give a reasonable time to reply.
- Fourteen days is common for claims against businesses. If a business is claiming a debt from an individual, the debt protocol requires at least 30 days.
- Attach or list the key documents: contract, invoices, photos, emails.
- Send it in a way you can prove, such as email plus recorded post, and keep copies.
- If there is no reply or no payment, you can usually start a claim online through Money Claim Online for amounts up to the small claims limit.

## What the law says in England and Wales

This section explains the general law. It is not advice on your situation.

- Before most court claims in England and Wales the **Practice Direction on Pre-Action Conduct** expects you to send a **letter before action** setting out the claim and giving the other side a reasonable time to reply.
- For debt claims by a business against an individual, the Pre-Action Protocol for Debt Claims applies and gives the debtor 30 days to respond.
- Claims up to £10,000 usually go to the small claims track, where costs are limited.
- Courts can penalise either side for ignoring the pre action steps, for example in costs.

## FAQ

**Do I have to send a letter before action?**

Yes in practice. The court can penalise a party who did not follow pre action steps, even if they win the **small claim**.

**How long do I give them to reply?**

Usually 14 days for a business. Longer may be needed for some claims, such as 30 days where a business claims a debt from an individual.

**They ignored my letter before action. What now?**

You can issue the claim. Keep proof the letter was sent. See the related wiki page on no response to a letter before action.
