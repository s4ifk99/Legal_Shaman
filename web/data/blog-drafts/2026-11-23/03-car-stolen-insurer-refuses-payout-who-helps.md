## Summary

**Does car insurance cover a stolen car?** Comprehensive and third party, fire and theft policies normally do. Third party only does not. If your **insurer refused the theft claim**, who helps starts with a written complaint to the insurer, then the Financial Ombudsman Service once you have a final response or eight weeks have passed. ## Key Information

- Report the theft to the police and get a crime reference number. Your insurer will ask for it.
- Check your policy schedule: **third party, fire and theft** and **comprehensive** cover theft; **third party only** does not.
- Common reasons for refusal are keys left in or near the car, an unlocked car, a security condition not met, or something not disclosed when you bought the policy. Ask for the exact clause in writing.
- If the insurer says you misrepresented something, the outcome depends on whether that was careless or deliberate. Ask them to explain which.
- You can usually take a complaint to the Financial Ombudsman within six months of the insurer's final response.

## What the law says in England and Wales

This section explains the general law. It is not advice on your situation.

- Whether **car insurance covers a stolen car** depends on the level of cover. Comprehensive and third party, fire and theft policies cover theft. Third party only does not.
- Insurers often refuse where a policy exclusion applies, for example keys left in the car or the car left unlocked. They must apply exclusions fairly and clearly.
- Under the **Consumer Insurance (Disclosure and Representations) Act 2012** you must take reasonable care to answer the insurer's questions accurately. An insurer can only refuse for a misrepresentation if it was careless or deliberate.
- If the insurer gives a final response or eight weeks pass, the Financial Ombudsman Service can review the refusal for free.

## Practical Guidance

- Get the refusal in writing with the policy clause the insurer relies on.
- Send a formal **complaint to the insurer** with your evidence (keys, photos, crime number, receipts).
- If there is no fix within eight weeks, or you get a final response you disagree with, go to the **Financial Ombudsman Service**.

## FAQ

**Does car insurance cover a stolen car?**

Yes on most comprehensive and **third party, fire and theft** policies, subject to the conditions. **Third party only** does not cover your own car being stolen.

**My insurer says I left the keys in the car. Can they refuse?**

Many policies exclude theft where keys were left in or on the car. Ask for the wording and the evidence. If you think it was applied unfairly, complain and then use the Ombudsman.

**How long does the insurer have to pay?**

There is no fixed deadline, but if the claim stalls for eight weeks after your complaint you can go to the Financial Ombudsman Service.
