## Summary

If you still get **nuisance calls** after registering with the **Telephone Preference Service (TPS)**, the companies calling may be breaking the law on marketing calls. Who helps: report the calls to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), use your phone provider's blocking tools, and report scam calls. The free doors below come from Legal Shaman Matching Help. They will **not** stop the calls for you or get you compensation.

## Key Information

- Registering with the **TPS** is free. It can take up to 28 days to take effect.
- Companies should not make live sales calls to TPS numbers unless you gave them consent.
- Automated recorded sales calls need your specific consent, whether or not you are on the TPS.
- Write down the number, date, time, company name and what they said. The ICO uses these reports to take action.
- Silent or abandoned calls are covered by Ofcom. Scam calls should be reported as fraud.

## Practical Guidance

- Check your number is on the **TPS** register and note the date you registered.
- Report each call to the **ICO** with the details you logged.
- Ask your phone provider about free call blocking, and report scam calls as fraud.

## FAQ

**Why am I still getting cold calls after registering with TPS?**

Some callers ignore the rules, some claim you gave consent, and some are scammers. Reporting to the ICO helps them build cases.

**Can I get compensation for nuisance calls?**

In some cases you can claim from the company directly, but most people simply report and block. A free adviser can tell you if a claim is realistic.

**What about spam texts?**

Forward spam texts to 7726 for free, then delete them.
