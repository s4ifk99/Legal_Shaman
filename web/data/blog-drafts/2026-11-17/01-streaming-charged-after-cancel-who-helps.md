## Summary

If a **streaming service charged after cancel**, who helps starts with cancel proof to the platform, then your card issuer or bank to stop further takes, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** cancel the subscription for you or sue the platform as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep the cancel confirmation, the notice period in the terms, and every bank line after the end date.
- App store and direct billing use different refund paths. Check which account took the money.
- Continuous payment authority and direct debit are stopped in different ways. Ask the bank for both stop and reclaim options.
- Write once to the trader with dates and amounts before you escalate.

## What Financial Ombudsman Service would actually do here

Look at whether a bank, insurer, or other financial firm handled a complaint fairly.

What they will **not** do: Will not take a case until you complained to the firm, and will not cover every non financial dispute.

## What Citizens Advice Adviceline (England) would actually do here

Explain your rights, point to templates, and route you to local or specialist help.

What they will **not** do: Will not chase the other party or represent you in court.

## Practical Guidance

- Send a dated complaint to the **streaming service** with cancel proof and the amounts you want back.
- Ask the **bank or card issuer** to stop future takes and open a chargeback path where it fits.
- **Financial Ombudsman Service**: [Open](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/).
- **Citizens Advice Adviceline (England)**: [Open](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/contact-us/advice-line/).

## FAQ

**They say the cancel only takes effect next billing cycle.**

Compare that claim with the written terms and your cancel timestamp. If they took money after the stated end date, ask for a refund of that take in writing.
