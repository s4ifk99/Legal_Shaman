## Summary

If **scooter hire** withheld your damage deposit, who helps starts with the checkout photos and a written dispute to the hire firm, then card reclaim where you paid by card, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** force the deposit back as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep pre hire and return photos, the damage report, and the deposit receipt.
- Ask for a dated breakdown of each alleged defect and the repair quote.
- Pre existing marks you photographed at pickup undercut a vague damage claim.
- Some apps use third party insurers. Note who actually holds the money.

- Dispute the charge in writing with the **hire company**, attaching before and after photos.
