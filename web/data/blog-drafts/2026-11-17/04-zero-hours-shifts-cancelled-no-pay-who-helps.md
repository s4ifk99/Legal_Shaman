## Summary

If **zero hours shifts** were cancelled last minute with no pay, who helps starts with a written chase to the employer or agency, then ACAS early conciliation where wages or holiday pay are disputed, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** force the rota to pay you as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep screenshots of the offered shifts, cancel messages, and every payslip.
- Worker versus employee status changes which rights apply. Note how you are engaged.
- Some contracts promise a minimum notice or a short cancel payment. Quote that clause.
- Unpaid holiday pay and unpaid hours already worked are separate money claims from future rota fights.

## Practical Guidance

- Write to **payroll or the agency** listing each cancelled shift date and what you want paid.
- Start **ACAS Early Conciliation** if wages or holiday pay stay unpaid after a clear chase.

## FAQ

**They say zero hours means no pay when shifts go.**

Ask for the contract clause in writing. If you already worked hours, or the contract promises cancel pay, chase those amounts as unpaid wages.
