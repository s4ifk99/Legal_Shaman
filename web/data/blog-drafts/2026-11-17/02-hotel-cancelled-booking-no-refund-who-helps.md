## Summary

If a **hotel cancelled** your booking and refused a refund, who helps starts with the booking intermediary (hotel, OTA, or package seller), then card or Section 75 where you paid by credit card, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** rebook the room for you or sue the hotel as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep the booking confirmation, cancel notice from the hotel, and every payment receipt.
- Who you contracted with matters: hotel direct, Online Travel Agent, or package organiser.
- Credit card **Section 75** can apply when the item cost is over £100 and within the usual card rules.
- Ask for a full cash refund, not only a voucher, unless you agree to a voucher in writing.

## What the law says in England and Wales

This section explains the general law. It is not advice on your situation.

- If the **hotel cancelled your booking**, it has not provided the service you paid for. You are normally entitled to a refund of what you paid.
- If you booked through an agent or booking site, check who the contract is with. The **Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018** give extra protection if the hotel was part of a package.
- Paying by credit card for a booking over £100 and up to £30,000 may give a claim under **Section 75** of the Consumer Credit Act 1974. Debit card payments may be covered by a **chargeback**.
- Keep the booking confirmation, the cancellation message and any extra costs you paid for replacement rooms.

## Practical Guidance

- Complain in writing to the **hotel or booking platform** named on the confirmation.
- If you paid by **credit card**, ask the issuer about Section 75 or chargeback with the same evidence pack.
- **Financial Ombudsman Service**: [Open](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/).
- **Citizens Advice Adviceline (England)**: [Open](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/contact-us/advice-line/).

## FAQ

**They only offered a voucher.**

You can refuse a voucher and keep asking for a refund of the money paid. Say so clearly in one dated email.
