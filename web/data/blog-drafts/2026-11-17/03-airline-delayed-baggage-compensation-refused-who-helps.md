## Summary

If an **airline delayed** your baggage and then refused compensation, who helps starts with the Property Irregularity Report and a written claim to the airline, then Alternative Dispute Resolution where the airline is signed up, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Those free doors will **not** recover the bags for you or sue the airline as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep the PIR or delayed bag reference, boarding passes, and receipts for essentials you had to buy.
- Deadlines for baggage claims are short. Check the airline complaint page and file early.
- Montreal Convention style limits often cap delayed bag claims. Receipts still matter.
- Separate flight delay rights from baggage rights. Do not mix the two claim packs.

## Practical Guidance

- File a dated complaint with the **airline** using the delayed bag reference and receipts.
- If the airline rejects you, use their named **ADR** scheme before court where one applies.

## FAQ

Check the date on the PIR and the claim email. If you filed inside their stated window, ask them to reopen with that proof attached.
