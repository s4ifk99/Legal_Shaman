## Summary

**Paternity discrimination at work** can include being treated worse because you took, or planned to take, paternity leave, or because of your sex in connection with becoming a father or partner. It can be unlawful under the Equality Act 2010 and under family leave protections. Who helps starts with a written timeline and a grievance, then ACAS early conciliation, then the free employment doors Matching Help returns. Time limits are short, so do not wait. Those doors will **not** run the tribunal claim as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Protection can apply to employees and, in some situations, workers. Many family leave rights have qualifying rules. Discrimination claims often do not need two years' service.
- Common examples: refused leave, lost shifts or a promotion, comments about taking time off, or dismissal after you told your employer you were taking paternity leave.
- Keep a timeline with dates, messages, rotas and who said what. Note when you told your employer about the birth or the leave.
- Before most tribunal claims you must contact ACAS for early conciliation. Tribunal time limits are short, usually three months less one day from the act. Check ACAS for the current rule.
- Speak to a solicitor if you have been dismissed, the deadline is close, or ACAS conciliation has ended.

## What the law says in England and Wales

This section explains the general law. It is not advice on your situation.

- The **Equality Act 2010** makes sex discrimination unlawful. Unfavourable treatment connected with being a father or partner, or with paternity leave, may fall within this and within family leave protections.
- Eligible employees have a statutory right to paternity leave and pay, with notice rules set out on GOV.UK and in ACAS guidance.
- Detriment or dismissal for taking, or seeking to take, paternity leave can be unlawful under the **Employment Rights Act 1996**.
- Tribunal claims usually must start within three months less one day, and you must contact ACAS for early conciliation first. The rules on time limits are changing, so check ACAS straight away.

## Practical Guidance

- Write a dated timeline and save emails, rotas and leave requests.
- Raise a written **grievance** with your employer, naming **paternity discrimination** or detriment for taking paternity leave.
- Contact **ACAS early conciliation** before the time limit, even if the grievance is still open.
- If you are in a union, contact your rep at the same time.

## FAQ

**What counts as paternity discrimination at work?**

Treatment that puts you at a disadvantage because of paternity leave, or because of your sex in connection with becoming a father or partner. Dismissal or a cut in shifts after you asked for leave is a common example.

**How long do I have to act?**

Usually three months less one day from the act to start ACAS early conciliation. The rules on time limits are changing, so check ACAS straight away.

**Do I need a solicitor?**

Not to start. The free doors can explain your rights. A solicitor is worth it for dismissal, large losses, or when the deadline is close.
