## Key Information

- Section 75 can apply to credit card payments over £100 for goods or services. Debit is usually chargeback under scheme rules.
- Companies House checks help you name the right company. Dissolution does not always end every claim path.
- Police are for clear fraud evidence. Most first steps stay civil and consumer.
