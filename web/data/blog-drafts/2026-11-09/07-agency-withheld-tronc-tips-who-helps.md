## Summary

If an **agency** withheld your **TRONC** tips after the hotel says it paid them on, who helps starts with a written payment trail ask to the agency, then the free employment doors Matching Help returns. Those doors will **not** chase payroll for you as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep timesheets, payslips, hotel confirmation that TRONC was paid to the agency, and app screenshots.
- Agency workers often have the agency as the employer for pay claims. Naming only the hotel can send you to the wrong door.
- Unpaid tips can sit beside a National Minimum Wage shortfall if tips were used to top up basic pay wrongly. Keep both angles separate in letters.

## Practical Guidance

- Write to the **agency** asking for the date and amount of each TRONC payment received from the client and paid to you.
- Copy the **hotel or venue** only to confirm what they remitted.
- Contact **ACAS** early conciliation if unpaid wages may go to tribunal.
- Use **HMRC** National Minimum Wage routes if pay looks below the minimum once tips are stripped out.
