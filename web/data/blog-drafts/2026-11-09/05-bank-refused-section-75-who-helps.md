## Summary

If your **bank refused a Section 75** claim, who helps next is a stronger evidence pack to the bank complaints team, then the free doors Matching Help returns (Financial Ombudsman and Citizens Advice on this run). Those doors will **not** guarantee a refund.

## Key Information

- Section 75 is a credit card route for qualifying goods or services over £100 where the issuer can share liability with the trader.
- Debit cards use chargeback, not Section 75. Check which product you actually used.
- Keep the credit agreement, merchant name on the statement, contract, and proof the trader failed.
- A first refusal often cites not eligible or trader still trading. Answer each point with documents.

## What Financial Ombudsman Service would actually do here

Look at whether a bank, insurer, or other financial firm handled a complaint fairly.

What they will **not** do: Will not take a case until you complained to the firm, and will not cover every non financial dispute.

## What Citizens Advice Adviceline (England) would actually do here

Explain your rights, point to templates, and route you to local or specialist help.

What they will **not** do: Will not chase the other party or represent you in court.

## Practical Guidance

- Write back to the **bank** complaints team answering each refusal reason with evidence.
- **Financial Ombudsman Service**: [Open](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/).
- **Citizens Advice Adviceline (England)**: [Open](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/contact-us/advice-line/).
