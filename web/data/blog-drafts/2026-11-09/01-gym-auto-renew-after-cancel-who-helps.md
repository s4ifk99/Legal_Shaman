## Summary

If a **gym** kept charging after you cancelled, who helps starts with your cancel proof to the gym, then your card issuer or bank to stop further takes, then the free doors Matching Help returns for this situation. Those free doors will **not** cancel the membership for you or sue the gym as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep the cancel email or app screenshot, the notice period clause, and every bank statement line after the end date.
- Direct debit and continuous card authority are different stop tools. Ask the bank how to end future takes as well as recover past ones.
- Cooling off rules can apply to distance or off premises contracts. Check dates before you rely on them.
- A membership sold in store may have different cancel rules from one sold online.

## Practical Guidance

- Send a dated complaint to the **gym** with cancel proof and the amounts you want back.
- Ask the **bank or card issuer** to stop future takes and open a chargeback or Section 75 path where it fits.
