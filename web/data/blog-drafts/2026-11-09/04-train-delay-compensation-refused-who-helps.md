## Summary

If a **train** company refused your Delay Repay style compensation, who helps starts with the operator complaint process and evidence of the delay, then the free doors Matching Help returns for this query. Do not skip the operator step. Free doors will **not** re book your journey for you.

## Key Information

- Keep tickets, booking references, delay screenshots, and the refusal email.
- Use the operator’s Delay Repay or complaints form before any ombudsman style door.
- Deadlines on the operator site matter. Late claims are often rejected on process alone.

## What Financial Ombudsman Service would actually do here

Look at whether a bank, insurer, or other financial firm handled a complaint fairly.

What they will **not** do: Will not take a case until you complained to the firm, and will not cover every non financial dispute.

## What Citizens Advice Adviceline (England) would actually do here

Explain your rights, point to templates, and route you to local or specialist help.

What they will **not** do: Will not chase the other party or represent you in court.

## Practical Guidance

- Resubmit or escalate inside the **train company’s** Delay Repay or complaints process with evidence.
- Note any ADR or rail ombudsman route named on the refusal letter and use that scheme only after their rules say you can.
- **Financial Ombudsman Service**: [Open](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/).
- **Citizens Advice Adviceline (England)**: [Open](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/contact-us/advice-line/).
