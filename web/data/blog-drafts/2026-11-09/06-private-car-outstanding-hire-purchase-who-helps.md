## Summary

If you **bought a car privately** and a lender says there is still **outstanding hire purchase**, who helps starts with your purchase evidence and a written hold ask to the finance company, then the free doors Matching Help returns. Advisers will **not** stop recovery agents at the door as your solicitor.

## Key Information

- Keep the advert, messages, bank transfer proof, V5C if you have it, and any receipt.
- Ask the lender for the agreement number and whether they treat the deal as hire purchase or conditional sale.
- An HPI style check before purchase can show finance markers. It does not by itself cancel a lender claim.
- Section 27 of the Hire Purchase Act 1964 can protect some private buyers. Conditions apply. Get advice before you rely on it.

## Practical Guidance

- Write to the **lender** asking for the legal basis of their claim and a hold on recovery while you take advice.
- Report to the **police** on 101 if you believe the seller committed fraud, and keep the crime number.
