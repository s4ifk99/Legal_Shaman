## Summary

For a **faulty sofa** where the retailer refuses a refund because more than 30 days have passed, who helps is still the retailer under the Consumer Rights Act path (repair, replace, then price reduction or reject), then **Citizens Advice** for templates, then alternative dispute resolution or small claims. Advisers will **not** collect the sofa or sue the shop for you.

## Key Information

- The short 30 day reject window is not the end of your rights if the sofa is not of satisfactory quality.
- Photograph defects, keep delivery notes, and write a dated complaint asking for repair or replacement first where that fits.
- “Sold as seen” or “no refunds” does not wipe Consumer Rights Act duties for most consumer sales.
- If the retailer stalls, ask whether they belong to a furniture ADR scheme before you start a claim.

## What Citizens Advice would actually do here

Advisers explain [faulty goods](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer/something-ive-bought-is-faulty-or-broken/) rights and point you to letter templates. They will not inspect the sofa, negotiate a cash settlement as your agent, or attend the hearing.

## What an ADR scheme would actually do here

If the retailer is in a furniture or retail ADR scheme, the scheme can propose a resolution after the retailer rejects your complaint. They will not force a criminal prosecution, or reopen once you have accepted a full settlement unless the rules allow.

## Practical Guidance

- Send a written complaint with photos and what you want (repair, replace, or refund after failed repair).
- Give a clear deadline. Keep screenshots of chat refusals.
- Call **Citizens Advice** if wording or next steps are unclear.
- Check ADR membership on letters or the retailer site.
- Card chargeback or Section 75 may still help if payment was by card and the goods are not as described.

## FAQ

**They say I am past 30 days so no refund.**
Rights can continue for repair or replacement, then further remedies. Get Citizens Advice wording before you accept that.

**Bought on finance?**
Complain to the retailer and tell the finance firm. Section 75 style routes may still matter.
