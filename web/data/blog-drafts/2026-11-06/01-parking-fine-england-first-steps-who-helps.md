## Summary

For a **parking fine** in England, who helps depends on the paper: a council or TfL **PCN** uses statutory representations then an independent tribunal; a private land charge uses the operator appeal then **POPLA** or **IAS**. **Citizens Advice** explains which path you are on and letter steps. Advisers will **not** cancel the charge for you or attend the hearing as your advocate.

## Key Information

- Check the logo and wording. “Penalty Charge Notice” from a council is not the same as a private “Parking Charge Notice”.
- Deadlines are short. Photograph the sign, bay, machine, and the full notice.
- Wrong registration, machine failure, and unclear signs are common appeal points. See sibling pages for those shapes.
- Do not ignore debt collector letters. Ask Citizens Advice how to reply.

## What Citizens Advice would actually do here

Advisers help you identify council versus private using [parking tickets](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer/cars/parking-tickets/) and [appealing a parking ticket](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/law-and-courts/parking-tickets/appealing-a-parking-ticket/). They point to appeal templates and explain escalation to POPLA, IAS, or the tribunal. They will not lodge the appeal account for you, or guarantee a win.

## What POPLA or IAS would actually do here

They decide a private charge after the operator rejects your first appeal (scheme membership required). They need the verification code from the rejection letter. They will not hear council PCNs, or reopen after a final decision just because you disagree.

## Practical Guidance

- Read every line of the notice: issuer, deadline, appeal URL.
- **Council / enforcement authority** informal challenge or formal representations if it is a statutory PCN.
- Independent **tribunal** (for example London Tribunals or Traffic Penalty Tribunal) after rejection, if eligible.
- **Private operator** appeal if it is private land.
- **POPLA** (BPA) or **IAS** (IPC) with the code after rejection.
- **Citizens Advice** if letters escalate or you are unsure which scheme applies.

## Services

| Who helps | What they actually do | What they do not do | How to reach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Council / TfL | Receive challenges and formal representations on PCNs | Not independent of the issuer | Address and portal on the PCN |
| Traffic tribunal | Independent hearing after formal rejection | Not for most private land charges | Link on the Notice of Rejection |
| Parking operator | First appeal on a private charge | Not independent | URL on the notice |
| POPLA / IAS | Independent private land appeal after rejection | Not for council PCNs | [POPLA](https://www.popla.co.uk/) · check notice for IAS |
| Citizens Advice | Explain the path; letter help | Will not cancel the ticket | [CA parking tickets](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer/cars/parking-tickets/) |
